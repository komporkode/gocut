# Privacy Policy for GoCut

**Last Updated:** November 2026

GoCut is a 100% offline desktop video editor developed with privacy as a foundational principle.

### 1. No Data Collection
GoCut does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, telemetry, tracking data, or usage statistics.

### 2. Completely Offline Operation
All media processing, video editing, local speech-to-text (Whisper AI), and project persistence occur entirely on your local computer. GoCut does not require an account, internet connection, or cloud services.

### 3. Media and Files
Your project files, imported videos, audio, images, and exported results remain exclusively on your local storage drives. GoCut never uploads or sends your media to external servers.

### 4. Contact
For questions regarding this policy or the software, open an issue on GitHub:
https://github.com/komporkode/gocut
